How the model works

The drawing shows one symmetric airfoil section. The readings estimate forces on a finite wing in steady, attached, low-speed flow. The animated lines illustrate flow direction and relative speed. They are schematic.

Angle sets the lift coefficient in this model. Drag coefficient includes a fixed baseline and a lift-dependent term. With angle held constant, doubling airspeed quadruples dynamic pressure, lift, and drag. Reynolds-number effects and stall are outside the model.

Assumptions: air density 1.225 kg/m³, wing area 10 m², aspect ratio 8, efficiency 0.85, baseline drag coefficient 0.020. Lift slope = 2π / (1 + 2π / (π × 8 × 0.85)) per radian. CD = 0.020 + CL² / (π × 8 × 0.85).
